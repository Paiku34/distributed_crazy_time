  ISTRUZIONI PER AVVIARE IL SISTEMA
  Distributed Crazy Time
========================================

1 terminale — RabbitMQ:

cd "C:\Program Files\RabbitMQ Server\rabbitmq_server-4.3.4\sbin"
.\rabbitmq-server.bat
aprire http://localhost:15672

2 terminale — Spring Boot (Java Gateway):

cd C:\Users\cacak\OneDrive\Desktop\distributed_crazy_time\java-gateway
mvn spring-boot:run

3 terminale — Erlang Game Engine:

cd C:\Users\cacak\OneDrive\Desktop\distributed_crazy_time\erlang-engine\game_engine
escript ..\rebar3 shell
  (il game engine parte automaticamente con supervisor, wheel_process, worker e 4 mini-giochi)

4 terminale — Curl per testare (comandi REST):

Registra utente:
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/register?username=testuser"

Controlla saldo:
curl.exe -X GET "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/balance?username=testuser"

Piazza scommessa su numero:
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/place-bet?username=testuser&amount=10.00&segment=5"

Piazza scommessa su mini-game:
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/place-bet?username=testuser&amount=5.00&segment=Pachinko"
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/place-bet?username=testuser&amount=5.00&segment=CoinFlip"
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/place-bet?username=testuser&amount=5.00&segment=CashHunt"
curl.exe -X POST "http://localhost:8080/api/wallet/place-bet?username=testuser&amount=5.00&segment=CrazyTime"
